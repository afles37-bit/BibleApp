About

Bible Verse Finder helps people locate half-remembered verses across approved Bible translations.

We focus on search quality, clear references, and fast results. The app currently searches the Amplified Bible (AMP), King James Version (KJV), and New Living Translation (NLT).

What are the brown italic words in the chapter view?

When you expand a chapter, some words appear in brown italic. These mark text that translators added to make the English flow naturally — words that are not present in the original Hebrew or Greek manuscripts.

The King James Bible traditionally printed these added words in italics for the same reason: to distinguish them from the underlying source text. Common examples include linking words like the, is, or be, as well as short phrases inserted to clarify who is speaking or what is being referred to.

These additions are not considered mistranslations. They reflect the translators' judgment about how to render ancient languages clearly in English, and they have been part of Bible translation practice for centuries.

Relevance scores

Each result shows a relevance score from 1 to 100. Higher scores mean more of your search words matched the verse text. A score of 100 means every word in your search appeared in the verse.
